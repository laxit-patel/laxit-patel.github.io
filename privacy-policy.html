This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: the app has no account and no server of ours. Your photos, videos and location records stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.

1. What the app does

The app takes photos and videos and stamps each one with the date, time, GPS coordinates, address, altitude, GPS accuracy and a small map. You can group shots into projects and export a project as a PDF report.

Location is read only while the app is open. The app does not track you in the background.

3. Address lookup

To turn coordinates into an address, the app uses your phone's built-in geocoding service. On most Android phones this is provided by Google, so your coordinates are sent to that service. This is handled by your phone's operating system and is subject to Google's privacy policy.

4. Map image

The map on the card is a picture loaded from the Google Maps Static API. To load it, the app sends the map's coordinates to Google, and Google receives your IP address as in any internet request. Google handles this under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. If you are offline, the card shows a pin without a map.

5. Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and device details to choose and measure ads, and may use cookies or similar identifiers. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, under Google, then Ads. Details: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

6. Where your photos and videos are stored

By default, photos and videos are kept in the app's private storage and do not appear in your phone's gallery. If you turn on "Also save to phone gallery" in Settings, a copy is also saved to your gallery, where other apps with media access can see it. The app does not back up its data to the cloud. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the private copies; gallery copies remain until you delete them.

7. Sharing

We do not receive your photos, videos or location. Files leave your phone only when you share them, for example by sending a photo or a PDF report through another app. What that app does with the file is governed by its own policy. We do not sell your information.

8. Analytics and tracking

The app has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own, and we do not build a profile of you. Google Play may give us general, anonymous statistics about installs and crashes through the Play Console.

9. Your choices

  • You can refuse or withdraw the camera, microphone and location permissions in your phone's Settings. The camera needs location to stamp coordinates; without it, shots have no location stamp.
  • You can delete any photo, video or project inside the app at any time.
  • You can reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

11. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage on your phone, protected by Android. Ads and the map image are loaded over secure connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.
